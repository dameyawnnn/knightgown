DESIGN SUMMARY

Overview

A structured, asymmetrical evening gown combining a sculpted, cap-sleeve bodice with a draped and folded cowl collar. The panelled bodice wraps diagonally across the torso, anchored by a geometric peplum with tiered, angular layers. A high-low, floor-length skirt falls in soft, draped tiers beneath the structured peplum. The back is finished with an open jute-mesh inset across the bodice and peplum, exposing a raw, textural contrast to the coated linen self fabric, and closes with a centre-back concealed zipper.

FRONT FEATURES
  • Asymmetrical folded collar
  • Panelled bodice
  • Structured gathers at peplum
  • Tiered skirt layers
  • High-low hem
  • Raw edge / fraying detail at hem
BACK FEATURES
  • Mesh inset panels across bodice
  • Centre-back concealed zipper
  • Reinforced seam details
  • Gathers at peplum
  • Hooded, folded collar with draped cowl
